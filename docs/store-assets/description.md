## Description (4000 chars max)

```
Cadence is a task and calendar app designed for how you actually live — not how productivity apps assume you should.

Your morning starts on the Today screen. A greeting, a progress ring, your timed tasks and events on one timeline, and a soft section for things you carried over from yesterday. No empty inboxes shouting at you. No twelve tabs to organize. Just what's on your plate.

Everything syncs through your private iCloud account. Cadence never sees your data — it can't. Sign in with Apple, and you're done.

═══════════════════════
TODAY, REIMAGINED
═══════════════════════
• Pinned tasks float to the top
• Timed tasks and calendar events share one timeline
• "Coming up" peeks at the next few days
• Carried Over tucks gracefully out of the way
• Daily progress ring that actually feels good

═══════════════════════
SPACES — SHARE WITH ANYONE
═══════════════════════
Spaces are private groups of lists you share with family, your team, your study group, or just yourself. Invite anyone with an iCloud account in two taps. They see only what you share — nothing else.

Perfect for "Joint Business," "Family Errands," "Wedding Planning," or whatever you're collaborating on this season.

═══════════════════════
CALENDAR THAT WORKS WITH YOU
═══════════════════════
• Connect Google Calendar (read-only, your tokens stay on-device)
• Day, Week, and Month views
• Drop tasks onto time slots
• Smart conflict detection
• Native iOS calendar support coming soon

═══════════════════════
FOCUS MODE
═══════════════════════
A clean Pomodoro timer with break tracking, completion sounds, and haptic feedback. Tracks your focus sessions automatically so you can look back at where your attention actually went.

═══════════════════════
HABITS AND RECURRING TASKS
═══════════════════════
Flag any recurring task as a habit. Cadence tracks streaks, surfaces them on Today, and never nags. Recurrence rules are real iCal-style (every weekday, last Friday of the month, every other Tuesday).

═══════════════════════
SIRI AND SHORTCUTS
═══════════════════════
"Hey Siri, add to Cadence: pay rent next Friday."
"Hey Siri, what's on my plate today?"
"Hey Siri, mark groceries done in Cadence."

Native App Intents work across iPhone, Watch (coming back soon), and Shortcuts.

═══════════════════════
DAILY REVIEW
═══════════════════════
At a time you pick, Cadence offers a 60-second evening review: what you finished, what carried over, an optional intention for tomorrow. No mandatory journaling.

═══════════════════════
DIFFERENT FROM…
═══════════════════════
• Things 3 — Things is gorgeous but doesn't sync calendars or share lists. Cadence does both.
• TickTick — TickTick is feature-rich but cluttered. Cadence keeps the surface calm.
• Apple Reminders — Reminders is fast but missing calendar integration, focus, habits, and shared groups beyond family. Cadence covers those.
• Sunsama / Motion — Beautiful but cost $20/mo and require a subscription. Cadence is free and stays on-device.

═══════════════════════
PRIVACY
═══════════════════════
• Your data lives in YOUR iCloud, not ours
• Sign in with Apple — no separate account
• No analytics. No tracking. No ads. No third-party SDKs collecting anything
• Open source — read the code at github.com/trippcarter/cadence-ios
• Full export to JSON, anytime
• Delete your account in two taps

═══════════════════════
WHO MADE THIS
═══════════════════════
Cadence is a solo project, made by one person for people who want a calm, beautiful place to plan their day. If you have feedback, the email in Settings → Help → Contact reaches a real human.

Welcome aboard.
```
