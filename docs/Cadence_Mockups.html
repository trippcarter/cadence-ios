Design system

Color and tone

A focused palette: violet for primary actions, teal for calendar, amber for warnings and "carried over", mint for completion. Everything else fades to a quiet neutral so content takes the stage.

About these mockups. These are static visual targets, not the final shipping UI — final spacing, typography, and motion will be tuned in SwiftUI with real device testing. The mockups demonstrate the visual direction: dark, calm, premium, with violet as the signature accent. A light theme will ship alongside (system default).

Working name. Cadence is a placeholder — Appendix A in the spec lists alternates. The accent color and design language work with any of them.

Tap to interact. The mockups are static here, but the implemented widgets will be interactive on iOS 17+ — tap a task on the Home Screen to complete it without opening the app.
